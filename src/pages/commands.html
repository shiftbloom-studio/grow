reference

Chat commands.

Everything your viewers can type, with an example for each. Numbers in brackets are optional and only count your own tasks, starting at 1. You can swap the ! for another symbol in the dashboard.

for everyone

Adding and finishing tasks

!task <text>also !add, !todo

Adds a task to the list under your name. Each viewer can have three open tasks by default.

viewer_one: !task read chapter 4
!done [n]also !check, !finish

Finishes your first open task, or your n-th task if you add a number.

viewer_one: !done 2
!undo [n]also !uncheck

Reopens the task you finished last, or your n-th task.

viewer_one: !undo
!edit [n] <text>

Changes the text of your latest open task, or of your n-th task.

viewer_one: !edit 1 read chapters 4 and 5
!remove [n]also !delete, !rm

Deletes one of your tasks.

viewer_one: !remove 3
!mytasksalso !tasks

Briefly highlights your tasks on the overlay, so you can find them on a long list.

viewer_one: !mytasks
for you and your mods

Cleaning up

!cleardone

Removes every finished task.

!clearuser @name

Removes all tasks of one viewer.

mod: !clearuser @viewer_two
!clearall

Empties the whole list. From the dashboard you get five seconds to undo it, from chat you don't.

If a command doesn't work, the overlay says why for a few seconds, for example when someone already has three open tasks. You can turn that off in the dashboard.

obs studio

Putting it in OBS

  1. Open the dashboard, make an overlay and copy its link.
  2. In OBS, click + under Sources, choose Browser and paste the link as the URL. The dashboard shows the width and height that fit your settings.
  3. Place it where you like. The background is transparent, so only the list shows. Streamlabs and other tools that have a browser source work the same way.
faq

Questions people ask

Do I need a bot account?
No. grow reads your chat anonymously, and only while your overlay is open.
Can viewers see or change my settings?
No. The overlay link can only read the list. Settings live in the dashboard, which needs your key.
What happens if my overlay link leaks?
Make a new overlay link in the dashboard. The old one stops working right away.
Does grow work with YouTube or Kick?
Not yet. grow reads Twitch chat. You can still add tasks yourself from the dashboard.
Is it free?
Yes. The hosted version is free, and the code is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later, so you can also run your own copy on Cloudflare.
