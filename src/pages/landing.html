OBS overlay · Twitch chat · free

Your stream's to-do list, growing live.

Viewers add goals with !task and tick them off with !done. You paste one link into OBS and watch the list fill up while you work together. Made for study, cowork and focus streams.

Start your overlay no bot account · no install

Three steps, one link.

01

Create

Open the dashboard, name your overlay and keep the key you get. That's your login.

02

Connect

Type your Twitch channel. grow listens to chat while your overlay is open, nothing else.

03

Stream

Add a Browser source in OBS, paste the link. Tasks appear the moment chat types them.

03 · details

Small tool, sharp edges.

  • Live everywhere. Every change reaches every overlay and your dashboard instantly.
  • Yours to style. Five themes, your accent color, size, auto-scroll and per-viewer limits.
  • Safe by default. The overlay link can only read. Rotate it any time and the old one stops working.
  • Open source. Read it, fork it, host it yourself. AGPL-3.0-or-later.
