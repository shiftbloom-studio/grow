Your chat has goals too.

grow puts a to‑do list on your stream that viewers fill from chat. They type !task to add something and !done when it's finished, and everyone watches the list shrink. It works best on study and cowork streams.

01 · setup

About five minutes, most of it in OBS.

01

Make an overlay

Give it a name and save the key you get. That key is your login.

02

Add your channel

Type your Twitch channel name. grow only reads chat while the overlay or dashboard is open.

03

Paste it into OBS

Add a Browser source and paste the overlay link. New tasks show up as soon as someone types them.

02 · commands

What chat can type.

!task read chapter 4adds a task (!add and !todo work too)
!done 2finishes your first open task, or task 2
!edit 1 new textrewrites task 1
!undo · !removereopens or deletes a task
!mytaskshighlights your tasks on stream
!cleardone · !clearuser @namemods and the streamer only

03 · details

The fine print.

  • The overlay link can only read. If it leaks, make a new one in the dashboard and the old one stops working.
  • There are five themes. Bloom matches this page, and Neon is for people who like neon.
  • You decide who can add tasks, from everyone down to mods only, and how many open tasks each viewer gets.
  • grow is open source under AGPL-3.0, and you can run your own copy on Cloudflare.
